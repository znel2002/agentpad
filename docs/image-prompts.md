# Showcase image prompts (Gemini "Nano Banana" / 2.5 Flash Image)

For the README hero + ship post. Generate 3-4, pick the cleanest (OLED text,
screw count, and keycap spelling vary run to run). Top-down angles render the
keycap legends most reliably.

## Hero shot (with keycap legends)

```text
Professional product-showcase render of a custom mechanical macropad called
"AgentPad", a compact desktop keypad for controlling AI coding assistants.

FORM FACTOR: a small sandwich-style macropad, roughly 70 x 90 mm, made of a
3D-printed matte case (top plate + body) with a visible green PCB edge. Six small
M3 hex screws recessed into the top plate corners and mid-edges. Clean, minimal,
premium build quality.

TOP CONTROL STRIP (above the keys): on the LEFT a small 0.91-inch monochrome OLED
display (wide, short, blue-on-black) showing two crisp lines of text:
"CLAUDE CODE" on the first line and "effort: HIGH" on the second line. On the
RIGHT a knurled aluminium rotary encoder knob.

KEY LAYOUT: a 3x3 grid of nine white DSA-profile keycaps, evenly spaced on
19.05 mm pitch. Each cap has a short label printed in the center in a clean bold
black uppercase sans-serif font, and each key is lit from beneath by a per-key
RGB LED so the cap glows a distinct color. The nine keys, left-to-right and
top-to-bottom, are:
- Row 1: "STOP" glowing red, "YES" glowing green, "NO" glowing amber
- Row 2: "PLAN" glowing blue, "NEW" glowing purple, "TALK" glowing cyan
- Row 3: "RUN" glowing white, "COMPACT" glowing teal, "LAYER" glowing warm amber
All labels must be spelled exactly as written, correct spelling, legible and sharp.
A soft RGB underglow spills from beneath the case onto the surface below.

BRANDING: the word "AgentPad" printed cleanly in a modern sans-serif on the top
plate along the front edge, subtle and legible.

SCENE: three-quarter hero angle, slightly above eye level, on a dark matte desk
surface with a softly blurred dark studio background. Cinematic soft key light
with gentle rim lighting, realistic reflections on the keycaps and knob, shallow
depth of field, high detail, 4k product photography, sharp focus on the pad.

STYLE: clean tech-product marketing render, like a premium keyboard brand's
website hero image. Photorealistic. No hands, no extra objects, no clutter.

Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

## Variants
- **Top-down flat lay** (best for legible legends): replace the SCENE paragraph with
  `perfectly top-down flat-lay view, centered, even soft lighting, dark textured desk, aspect ratio 1:1.`
- **Lifestyle:** replace SCENE with `on a developer's wooden desk beside a glowing monitor showing a code terminal, warm ambient evening light, bokeh background, aspect ratio 16:9.`
- **Exploded view:** add `shown as an exploded assembly diagram with the top plate, keycaps, PCB, and bottom case floating apart in stacked layers.`

## Fixups
- Fake letters on caps? add `blank keycaps with no letters` (or keep the labeled version).
- Long word breaking? shorten `COMPACT` -> `CMPCT` in the prompt.
- Iterate: feed the winner back with "same macropad, keep the green PCB and
  color-coded labeled keys, now show it from a lower front angle."
